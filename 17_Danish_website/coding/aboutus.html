The Adidas Story

Adidas was founded by Adolf “Adi” Dassler in Germany. His goal was simple: to create sports shoes that helped athletes perform better. Over time, Adidas became known for its three stripes, innovative footwear, and strong connection to sports, streetwear, and youth culture.

Today, Adidas is recognised around the world for combining performance, comfort, and style. The brand continues to create products for athletes, creators, and everyday people who want to move with confidence.

Brief History

1949

Adidas was officially founded by Adi Dassler in Germany.

1950s

The brand became popular in sports because of its performance footwear.

1970s

Adidas expanded into clothing, football gear, and global sports culture.

Today

Adidas is known for sportswear, sneakers, lifestyle fashion, and innovation.
